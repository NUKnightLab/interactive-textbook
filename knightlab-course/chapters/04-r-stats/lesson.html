Chapter 4: R

What's in analyze.R

Open analyze.R on the right. It does three things:

  1. Loads iris.csv from the course datasets.
  2. Groups by species and computes the average petal and sepal length.
  3. Makes a ggplot2 scatter plot and saves it as a PNG to student_work/iris_plot.png.
